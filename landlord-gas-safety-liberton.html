Landlord Gas Safety Certificate FAQs for Liberton Residents

If you rent out a property with gas appliances, pipework or flues, yes. The Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 require a landlord to have each appliance and flue checked every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and to keep a record. The record is commonly called a landlord gas safety certificate or CP12.

Yes. We check rental properties across Liberton and the surrounding EH16 area, including Liberton Road, Kirk Brae and Alnwickhill Road.

Every 12 months. You can have the check done in the two months before it is due and keep the original renewal date, so you do not lose time by being early.

For two years, or until the next check is done and recorded. Keep a copy somewhere safe, and send one to your agent if you use one.

We tell you what is wrong. Where an appliance is unsafe, it needs to be made safe or disconnected, and the record notes the defect and what was done. We explain what is needed and, where you want us to, quote for the work.

For a typical house with a boiler and a gas hob, it is often in the region of an hour, depending on the number of appliances, access and any issues found. We will give you a better idea when we know what is in the property.
