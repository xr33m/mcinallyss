What We Encounter in Bruntsfield

Pipe Emergencies We Repair in Bruntsfield

A burst is not always where the water shows. Here is what we most often find in Bruntsfield.

Leak Coming Through the Ceiling Below

Water in a tenement travels. Stop the supply, switch off nearby electrics if it is safe, and call us so we can trace the source.

Water Meter Moving With Everything Off

If the meter keeps turning with every tap off, there is a leak somewhere. We trace it.

Shared or Hard-to-Find Stopcock

In older buildings the stopcock can be shared or hidden. We help you isolate the water and find the right valve.

Pipe Split After a Thaw

A pipe that froze may split, and only start leaking as it thaws. We check carefully and repair as needed.

Frozen Pipes, No Water

If your water has stopped in freezing weather, a pipe may be frozen. We thaw it safely and check for splits.

Old Lead or Iron Pipework Failing

Older buildings can have ageing pipework that splits or corrodes. We repair the failure and advise on replacing weak sections.
