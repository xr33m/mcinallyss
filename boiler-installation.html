Signs You Need a New Boiler

Signs It's Time for Replacement, Not Another Repair

Not sure whether to repair or replace? These are the signals we weigh up on every visit.

Boiler Is 12+ Years Old

Most domestic boilers have a working life of 10-15 years. Beyond that, efficiency drops and part failures become more frequent and expensive.

Repeated Breakdowns

If you've called out an engineer more than once this heating season, the cost of continued repairs often exceeds the cost of replacement within a year or two.

Parts Becoming Hard to Source

Older or discontinued models can mean longer waits for parts and higher costs when they are available at all.

Rising Energy Bills

An ageing, inefficient boiler works harder to deliver the same heat output. A modern condensing boiler can cut heating costs noticeably.

Not Keeping Up With Demand

Extended your home, added a bathroom, or just find hot water runs out too fast? Your existing boiler may simply be undersized for current usage.

Uneconomical to Repair

When a single repair quote approaches a meaningful fraction of a new boiler's cost, replacement is almost always the better long-term investment.

Boiler Types

Boiler Types We Install in Edinburgh

We size and recommend the right system for your property — not a one-size-fits-all swap.

Combi Boilers

Heating and hot water from a single unit with no tank required. Ideal for flats and smaller households needing instant hot water.

System Boilers

Paired with a hot water cylinder, suited to homes with higher hot water demand or multiple bathrooms running at once.

Regular (Heat-Only) Boilers

Suited to period properties retaining an existing heating layout, cylinder and tanks — often the simplest, least disruptive replacement.
