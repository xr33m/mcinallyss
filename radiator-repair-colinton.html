Sludge build-up in Colinton's older heating systems causes cold spots and poor performance. We power flush systems to clear this properly.

Seized TRV Pins Causing Overheating

Stuck thermostatic radiator valve pins are a common fault in Colinton homes, often causing radiators to overheat or not respond to the thermostat. We free or replace these.

Air-Locked Radiators

Trapped air causes cold tops and gurgling noises. We bleed and, where the problem persists, investigate the underlying cause across Colinton systems.

Leaking Radiator Valves

A leaking valve can cause damage over time. We repair or replace faulty valves throughout Colinton properties.

Unbalanced Heating Systems

Some rooms too hot, others too cold — this usually means the system needs balancing. We rebalance heating systems across Colinton for even warmth throughout.

Noisy Heating Pipes
