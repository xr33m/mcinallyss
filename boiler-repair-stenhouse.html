What Goes Wrong With Boilers in Stenhouse

The cause of a breakdown is not always the boiler. Here are the faults we most often track down in Stenhouse.

Boiler Making Strange Noises

Banging, kettling or whistling can mean limescale, trapped air or poor flow. Worth fixing before it becomes a breakdown.

Boiler Running Noisily

Kettling or banging can mean limescale or poor flow. It is cheaper to deal with early than after a breakdown.

Boiler Cuts Out Randomly

Intermittent shutdowns can be low pressure, overheating, poor flow or a thermostat fault. We work through them methodically.

No Hot Water

Heating works but no hot water, or the other way round? That usually points to a diverter valve, pump or controls fault.

Older Pipework, Newer Boiler

A newer boiler on older pipework can suffer from sludge and poor flow. We tell you whether it is the boiler or the system.

No Heating or Hot Water

When a boiler stops altogether, we start with the basics (gas, power, pressure, controls) and work through to the fault.
