Boiler Installation — New Town

New Town's Boiler Installation Specialists

New Town's Georgian townhouses and flats — on streets like Heriot Row, Great King Street, Queen Street, Drummond Place and India Street — are largely within a UNESCO World Heritage Site and conservation area. That means flue placement, external alterations and even some internal work can be subject to listed building consent, and it means shared common stairs are often the only access route to upper-floor flats.

McInally's has fitted new boilers throughout New Town's period stock. We check conservation area and listed building status before quoting, plan flue routing that respects the building's facade, and arrange common-stair access with minimal disruption to neighbours. Local Property Knowledge

Boiler Installation in New Town Properties

Many New Town buildings are listed and sit within Edinburgh's premier conservation area, which means an external flue on a principal facade is often refused. Where this applies, we route the flue internally or to a rear elevation, agreeing the approach with you at survey stage rather than discovering the restriction on installation day.

New Town's tall townhouses — often four or five storeys including basement and attic flats — have larger room volumes and longer pipe runs than a typical modern property. We calculate heat loss across the whole building rather than assuming a standard combi will cope, and we're comfortable working with factors to arrange common-stair access for upper-floor and basement flats alike.

New Town Installation Considerations

What We Plan For on Every New Town Installation

These are the recurring factors that shape a boiler installation in New Town's period buildings.

Listed Building & Conservation Status

We check whether your building is listed or sits within the New Town conservation area before quoting, and plan flue placement accordingly.

Common Stair Access

Many New Town flats are only reachable via a shared stair. We arrange access with your factor and time deliveries to minimise disruption to neighbours.

Internal Flue Routing

Where an external flue on a principal facade isn't permitted, we route through a rear wall or existing chimney breast where suitable.

Sizing for Tall Townhouses

Four and five-storey townhouses have larger heat loss than a standard flat. We calculate this properly rather than fitting an undersized system.

Basement & Attic Flat Access

New Town conversions often include basement (garden) and attic flats with their own access challenges — we plan logistics for these at survey stage.

Older Existing Pipework

Original gas and heating pipework in unrenovated New Town flats sometimes needs partial upgrading to support a modern boiler safely — we flag this upfront.
