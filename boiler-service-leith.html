Leith Boiler Faults

Common Boiler Problems We Solve in Leith

These are the boiler and heating faults we diagnose and fix most often across EH6.

Boiler Not Firing Up

Failed ignition, pilot light faults, or gas supply issues — diagnosed quickly with common parts carried on the van.

Losing Pressure

Recurring pressure loss is usually a faulty expansion vessel, worn PRV, or a small leak — we find and fix the root cause.

Radiators Not Heating Evenly

Sludge build-up in older Leith heating systems causes cold patches — we offer power flushing to restore full circulation.

Unusual Noises

Kettling or banging usually points to limescale or a worn pump — diagnosed and repaired to restore quiet operation.

High Energy Bills

An unserviced boiler works harder and costs more to run — an annual service restores efficiency.
