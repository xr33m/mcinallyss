Right-Sized Boilers for The Grange's Larger Homes

The Grange's Victorian villas — on streets like Grange Loan, Grange Road, Dick Place, Findhorn Place and Lauder Road — are typically larger than average, with more radiators, bigger hot water demand, and older heating pipework. A straightforward like-for-like boiler swap can leave a system undersized for the property.

Local Property Knowledge

Boiler Installation Alongside Central Heating Upgrades

Many Grange properties benefit from combining a new boiler with a central heating upgrade — new radiators, updated pipework, or a power flush — in a single visit rather than as separate jobs. We'll flag this at survey stage if it applies to your property, rather than leaving you to discover the limitation after the boiler's already fitted.

With multiple bathrooms and higher simultaneous hot water demand than a typical flat, many Grange villas are better served by a system boiler and cylinder rather than a combi. We calculate this properly rather than defaulting to the cheapest option.

The Grange Installation Considerations

What We Plan For on Every Grange Installation

These are the recurring factors that shape a boiler installation in The Grange's larger period homes.

Sizing for Larger Properties

We calculate actual heat loss and hot water demand for the whole villa rather than assuming a standard combi will cope.

Multiple Bathroom Demand

Villas with two or more bathrooms often need a system boiler and cylinder to cope with simultaneous hot water use.

Older Pipework & Radiators

Original pipework sized for an older, lower-efficiency system can bottleneck a new boiler's performance — we check this before quoting.

Combining Works in One Visit

Where a heating upgrade is needed alongside the boiler, we combine both into a single, coordinated job rather than two separate visits.

Garden Rooms & Extensions

Grange properties with garden room conversions or extensions need heating demand recalculated to include the additional space.

Basement & Cellar Plant Rooms

Many Grange villas have a basement plant room suited to a larger system boiler — we assess access and ventilation as part of the survey.
