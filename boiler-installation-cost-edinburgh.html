How Much Does a New Boiler Cost in Edinburgh?

There is no single price for a new boiler. The cost depends on the boiler you choose, the size your home needs, and what the job involves. This guide explains what moves the price, what a proper quote should include, and how to compare quotes fairly.

What decides the price of a boiler installation

Two homes can need very different work even when both are "just a boiler swap". These are the factors that change the quote:

  • Boiler type. A combi heats water on demand and needs no tank. A system boiler works with a hot water cylinder. A regular (heat-only) boiler works with a cylinder and a loft tank. Switching from one type to another means extra pipework and often removing tanks.
  • Output (kW). The boiler must match your heating and hot water demand. A one-bedroom flat and a five-bedroom house need different outputs, and a bigger boiler is not automatically a better one.
  • Brand and model. Different manufacturers and ranges carry different prices and different guarantee lengths.
  • Position and flue. Fitting the new boiler where the old one was is the simplest job. Moving it, or changing the flue route, adds labour and materials.
  • System condition. A heating system full of sludge may need cleaning or a filter fitted so the new boiler is protected.
  • Controls. A basic thermostat, a programmable one and a smart control all cost different amounts.
  • Property. Tenement flats, older stone houses and newer builds each bring their own access and flue considerations. We check these at the survey.

What a proper boiler quote should include

When you compare quotes, check every one lists the same things. A low headline price that leaves items out is not a lower price.

  • The exact boiler make, model and output
  • Removal and disposal of your old boiler
  • Flue, fittings and any new pipework
  • Whether a system flush and a filter are included
  • The controls being supplied
  • Commissioning and your Gas Safe and Benchmark paperwork
  • Manufacturer guarantee length and what it needs from you (for example, annual servicing)
  • Who will do the work, and their Gas Safe registration
  • Timescale, and what happens if the job finds extra work

How to compare quotes, including from large national firms

People often ask how much a national company charges to fit a boiler. We don't quote other companies' prices because they change and depend on the home. A better approach is to ask each company for a written, itemised quote for the same boiler type and output, then compare line by line. Ask whether the engineer who surveys your home is the engineer who fits the boiler.

Is a new boiler always the answer?

Not always. If your boiler is fairly new and the fault is isolated, a repair may make more sense. Our guide on whether to replace an old boiler explains how to decide. You may also be able to get help with the cost: read boiler grants in Scotland.
