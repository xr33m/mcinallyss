Local Property Knowledge

Combi vs System Boilers in Merchiston Flats

Smaller Merchiston flats with one bathroom are usually well suited to a combi boiler — no tank, instant hot water. Larger terraced houses or flats with multiple bathrooms often perform better with a system boiler and cylinder. We'll recommend the right fit rather than defaulting to the cheapest option.

Where a property has been converted from a single villa into flats, gas supply pipework can be older and shared in ways that need checking before a new boiler goes in. We trace and assess this as part of every survey.

Merchiston Installation Considerations

What We Plan For on Every Merchiston Installation

These are the recurring factors that shape a boiler installation in Merchiston's terraces and conversions.

Combi vs System Choice

We assess your actual hot water demand rather than assuming — smaller flats usually suit combi, larger terraces often need a system boiler.

Older Gas Runs

Some conversion flats have gas pipework sized for older, lower-output boilers — we check and upgrade this where required.

Shared Building Flues

Converted villas sometimes have shared or complex flue routes between flats — we plan compliant routing before quoting.

Tenement Stair Access

Where a flat is only reachable via a shared stair, we coordinate delivery and access timing to minimise disruption.

Napier Campus Area Rentals

Merchiston's proximity to Edinburgh Napier University means a number of rental conversions — we work with landlords on efficient, low-maintenance installations.

Existing Airing Cupboard Tanks

Converting from a regular boiler to combi means removing tanks and cylinders — we handle this cleanly as part of the installation.
