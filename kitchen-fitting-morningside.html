Kitchen Fitting in Morningside Homes

In Morningside's Victorian villas, original pipework often runs beneath solid timber floors or through internal walls not designed for modern appliance loads. We plan layouts that work with these constraints, lifting and relaying floorboards carefully where access is needed.

Conversion flats carved out of larger villas sometimes have kitchen plumbing that was adapted at the point of conversion — we map the existing system before proposing changes, so nothing gets disrupted unnecessarily elsewhere in the building.

What We Manage

What a Morningside Kitchen Refit Involves

Every Morningside kitchen job is different — these are the recurring factors we plan for.

Original Pipework Beneath Floors

Victorian villa kitchens often have pipework beneath solid timber floors — we access this carefully and relay boards neatly afterwards.

Working Around Period Features

Cornicing, original tiling, and other period details are worked around wherever possible rather than removed.

Conversion Flat Plumbing

Kitchens in villa conversions sometimes share systems adapted during the original conversion — we map these before making changes.

High-Ceilinged Layouts

Taller Morningside kitchens can accommodate different cabinet and extraction layouts than standard new-builds — we advise on what works best.

Appliance Connections

Integrated dishwashers, washing machines and American-style fridges connected correctly as standard.
