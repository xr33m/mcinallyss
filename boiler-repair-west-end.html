Common Boiler Faults in West End

The cause of a breakdown is not always the boiler. Here are the faults we most often track down in West End.

Boiler Making Strange Noises

Banging, kettling or whistling can mean limescale, trapped air or poor flow. Worth fixing before it becomes a breakdown.

Boiler Showing a Fault Code

Tell us the code and the make and model. It is often the fastest route to the cause.

Heating Layout Not Built for a Boiler

Boilers fitted into older heating layouts can struggle with long pipe runs. We look at the system before blaming the unit.

Radiators Cold or Slow to Heat

Cold radiators can be air, sludge or a failing pump or valve. We work out whether it is the boiler or the system.

Losing Pressure Over Days

A slow pressure drop in an older building can mean a pinhole leak somewhere in the pipework as well as a boiler fault.

Boiler Cutting Out Mid-Cycle

In a large, older system a boiler can overheat or lose flow and shut itself down. We check circulation as well as the boiler.
