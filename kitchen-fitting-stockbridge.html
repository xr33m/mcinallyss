What a Stockbridge Kitchen Refit Involves

Every Stockbridge kitchen job is different — these are the recurring factors we plan for.

Original Lead or Early Copper Pipework

Older Stockbridge properties sometimes retain original lead or early copper supply pipes — we assess and replace where needed as part of the refit.

High Finish Expectations

Stockbridge properties call for a higher standard of tiling, worktop sealing and appliance integration — we hold every job to this bar.

Period Feature Preservation

Original cornicing, skirting and period details are worked around carefully rather than removed.

Shared Soil Stacks in Conversions

Some Stockbridge conversions share drainage — we plan sink and dishwasher positioning around this.

Appliance Connections

Integrated dishwashers, washing machines and American-style fridges connected correctly as standard.
