When to Call

Signs You Need a New or Upgraded Hot Water Cylinder

These are the hot water problems Edinburgh homeowners most often call us about.

Weak Hot Water Pressure

A loft tank relies on gravity, so showers can be weak, especially on upper floors. An unvented cylinder delivers hot water at mains pressure.

Running Out of Hot Water

An undersized or failing cylinder runs out quickly. We size the replacement to the number of bathrooms and the people in the home.

Cylinder Leaking or Corroded

Water at the base, damp lagging or weeping joints mean the cylinder is failing. Do not wait for it to fail completely. See our leak repair service.

Dripping From the Discharge Pipe

Constant dripping from the pipe below an unvented cylinder means a safety device or the supply pressure needs checking. Never block or cap the pipe.

Slow to Heat or High Bills

An old, poorly insulated cylinder, a scaled coil or a failed immersion heater or thermostat wastes energy and leaves you waiting for hot water.

Loft Tank in the Way

Planning a loft conversion, or tired of tanks taking up space? An unvented cylinder removes the cold water storage tank for your hot water.

Unvented Cylinders: What to Know Before You Buy

What an unvented cylinder is

It is a sealed hot water store fed directly from the mains. Because it needs no cold water tank, you get mains-pressure hot water at every outlet. The trade-off is that a pressurised cylinder needs proper safety equipment and a good incoming water supply.

The safety devices

Scottish building standards guidance for unvented systems includes an expansion valve to relieve excess pressure and an expansion vessel or other means of accommodating expanded water, alongside the cylinder's thermostatic controls. In practice that means a pressure reducing valve, an expansion vessel, a temperature and pressure relief valve and a tundish with a safe discharge pipe. None of these should ever be removed, blocked or bypassed.

Who should fit it

The same guidance says the installer should be a competent person, and that the installation should be labelled to identify them. You can read the wording in the Scottish Building Standards technical handbook (danger from heat). Ask any installer for commissioning paperwork when the job is finished.

Your water supply

Manufacturers typically recommend a minimum of about 1.5 bar working pressure and 20 litres a minute, although some ask for more or less. A high static pressure on its own does not guarantee good flow, which is why we measure both. Not sure whether a combi or a cylinder suits your home? Read combi, system or regular boiler: which to choose.

Servicing

Annual inspection is generally recommended, and many warranties expect it. A service checks the valves, the expansion vessel, the operating pressure and the discharge pipework. Pair it with your annual boiler service and you only need one visit.
