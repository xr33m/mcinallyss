Landlord Gas Safety: What to Know

The duty

Landlords who let a property with gas appliances, pipework or flues must have them checked every 12 months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, keep a record, and give tenants a copy. The Health and Safety Executive publishes guidance on landlords' responsibilities.

The record

The Landlord Gas Safety Record, commonly called a CP12, lists each appliance and flue checked, the results and any defects. Keep it for two years, and give a copy to the tenant within 28 days of the check or before a new tenant moves in.

Scotland

In Scotland the Repairing Standard also requires heating and hot water installations to be in reasonable repair and proper working order, and carbon monoxide detection rules apply where there is a fuel-burning appliance. Check the Scottish Government's landlord guidance.

Service versus safety check

A boiler service is about performance. A gas safety check confirms that appliances and flues are safe. We can do both in one visit.
